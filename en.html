Welcome

A stylish and lovingly renovated 69 m² apartment in beautiful Porto Cristo, Mallorca.

A traditional “Patio de Luz” brings additional daylight into the apartment and connects the living room with bedroom and bathroom.

The bedroom offers a comfortable double bed and a relaxing atmosphere.
