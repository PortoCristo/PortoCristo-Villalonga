Welcome

We are delighted to welcome you here. Our Villalonga holiday apartment is located in the heart of Porto Cristo – just a one-minute walk from the beach.

The 69 m² apartment has been renovated and furnished with great attention to detail. A bright living area, a cosy dining corner and the open-plan American-style kitchen come together to create a spacious and pleasant home.

Two bedrooms, a modern bathroom and the centrally located Patio de Luz make the apartment a special place. The traditional interior also brings plenty of daylight into the apartment and connects the individual rooms with one another.

It is important to us that during your stay you do not simply stay in a holiday apartment, but genuinely feel at home. That is why we place particular emphasis on cleanliness, comfort and personal contact with our guests.
