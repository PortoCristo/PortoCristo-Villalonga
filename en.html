Welcome

to our lovingly renovated and stylish holiday apartment in Porto Cristo

This bright 69 m² apartment was originally two separate spaces and has been transformed into an open-plan living concept, offering a light-filled and pleasant atmosphere for up to two guests.

The heart of the apartment is the open living area with an American-style kitchen, dining area and comfortable lounge. Large windows and smart layout provide plenty of natural light and a welcoming atmosphere. The elegant parquet flooring enhances the stylish character of the apartment.

A traditional patio de luz brings additional daylight into the apartment and connects the living area with the bedroom and bathroom. The bedroom features a comfortable double bed and offers plenty of space for a relaxing stay.
