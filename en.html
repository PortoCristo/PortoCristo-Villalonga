Welcome

in our lovingly renovated and stylishly furnished holiday apartment in beautiful Porto Cristo

This bright 69 m² apartment was originally converted from two separate rooms into an open living concept, creating a very bright and pleasant atmosphere for up to two guests.

The heart of the apartment is the open living area with an American-style kitchen, a cozy dining area and a comfortable lounge. Large windows and a smart layout provide plenty of natural light and a welcoming atmosphere. The elegant parquet flooring emphasizes the stylish character of the apartment.

A traditional patio de luz brings additional daylight into the apartment and connects the living area with the bedroom and bathroom. The bedroom offers a comfortable double bed and plenty of space for a pleasant stay.
