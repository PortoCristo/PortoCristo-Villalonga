Welcome

in our lovingly renovated and stylish holiday apartment in beautiful Porto Cristo

This bright 69 m² apartment was originally converted from two separate rooms into a spacious open-plan living concept and now offers a particularly bright and pleasant atmosphere for up to two guests.

The heart of the apartment is the open living area with an American-style kitchen, a cozy dining area and a comfortable lounge. Large windows and a well-thought-out layout create plenty of natural light and an inviting atmosphere. The elegant parquet flooring further emphasizes the stylish character of the apartment.

A traditional light patio brings additional daylight into the apartment and connects the living area with the bedroom and bathroom. The bedroom features a comfortable double bed and offers enough space for a pleasant stay.
